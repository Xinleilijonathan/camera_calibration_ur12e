What the camera_3 session (2026-09-26) taught us, which this set predates:

  * The px gate is scale-dependent and ranks these two cameras BACKWARDS.
    camera_2 sits at 0.83 m median (1 px = 0.923 mm); camera_3 at 0.98 m
    (1 px = 1.077 mm). By the real mm budget camera_2 is the BETTER of the
    two -- 2.58 mm hold-out vs camera_3's 4.25 mm -- yet camera_2 fails the
    px gate and camera_3 passes it. Depth error along the viewing ray barely
    shows up in reprojection, so the further camera looks better in px while
    being worse in 3D. Same argument as the outlier_limits comment in
    config/calibration.yaml, now with cross-camera evidence.

  * The "five solvers disagree" warning is driven by andreff alone. Here the
    other four agree to 3.51 mm; on camera_3, to 4.29 mm. Read the spread
    with andreff excluded before suspecting degeneracy.
